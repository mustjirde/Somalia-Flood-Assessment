# Project catalogue

This catalogue consolidates the technical work represented in the user's ChatGPT and local project workspaces. It is intentionally written as a public-safe portfolio summary.

## 1. El Niño Flood Assessment 2026

**Domain:** remote sensing, hydrology, emergency mapping

**Methods:** Google Earth Engine Sentinel-1 VV acquisition; pre/post backscatter comparison; ArcPy raster processing; district-level zonal statistics; map and report export.

**Outputs:** flood-extent raster, district flooded-area table, CSV statistics, and situation maps.

## 2. River Breakages Assessment

**Domain:** riverine flood monitoring

**Methods:** satellite-image interpretation, point-based observation/status/width assessment, image-gap and cloud handling, and audit-ready field tables.

**Outputs:** validated river-breakage observations and supporting imagery/audit records.

## 3. Somali Climate Service

**Domain:** climate services and early warning

**Methods:** climate-information workflow design, stakeholder-oriented products, proposal/work-plan support, and decision-support communication.

## 4. Hirshabelle and Somalia mapping

**Domain:** cartography and regional analysis

**Methods:** administrative mapping, flood context, thematic layers, layouts, and map-product preparation.

## 5. LIDAR and terrain processing

**Domain:** elevation and terrain analysis

**Methods:** DEM preparation, mosaicking, geospatial data organization, and ArcGIS automation.

## 6. Land-cover validation

**Domain:** land monitoring and quality control

**Methods:** desktop validation, structured assignments, image review, and repeatable evidence capture.

## 7. Weather forecast mapping

**Domain:** operational forecasting

**Methods:** repeatable forecast-map production, local workflow automation, and reporting.

## 8. Soil Survey and IBLI

**Domain:** soils, rangelands, and climate-risk finance

**Methods:** field/research organization, spatial support, and index-based livestock-insurance analysis.

## 9. Hal-Abuur climate baseline

**Domain:** climate baseline and land-use analysis

**Methods:** baseline assessment, land-use/land-cover context, and climate-consulting support.

## Public-safe publishing rule

Only code, generic documentation, synthetic examples, and non-sensitive metadata should be published. Do not commit private imagery, client deliverables, credentials, personal records, proposal attachments, or proprietary datasets.
